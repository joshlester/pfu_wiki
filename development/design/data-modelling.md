
# Data Modelling

Facts & Dimensions
`Facts` are generally quantitative e.g. Sales Amount or Sales Quantity.
`Dimensions` are Nouns or Time (e.g. Year, month etc.). Dimensions describe how the data should be sliced e.g. Give me total sales in January for store Y.
